# Brand content implementation

Source: Figma Slides mW7FMh6nuOiW5HsT9e7ElD, final slides 1:8 (Key Message & Audience) and 1:10 (Creative Direction), read September 24, 2026.

Implemented:
- Home: ingredient-first introduction connecting origin, formulation and daily use, alongside the established collection and scroll campaign.
- why-fewer.html: botanical inspiration, scientific precision, quiet luxury, a focused routine and transparency, adapted as public-facing brand principles rather than verified efficacy claims.
- journal.html: editorial index linking to real articles; future founding ingredient and expert editorial programme clearly labelled as pending.
- ingredient-to-formula.html: editorial on identity, origin and purpose; explicitly does not invent a founding ingredient.
- fingertip-moments.html: human-touch photographic series using the supplied campaign image, connecting to the planned social visual direction. No social account created or publication sent.
- traceability.html: current-state records for each displayed product, with direct links from product detail dialogs; distinct ingredient, source, evidence, batch and unit-number statuses.
- Home header logo: original Figma PNG revealed through an SVG mask following the existing emblem, followed by the wordmark. This is a raster reveal, not recreated vector artwork. Once per tab; no blocking loader; respects reduced motion.
- All pages retain one shared fixed background, original logo, brand colours, and common navigation.

Still requiring authoritative brand input:
- Founding ingredient identity, botanical part, supplier, source region, ingredient lists and product-specific evidence.
- Production batch records, unit numbering rules and actual assigned numbers. Deck example 0032/500 is not a real product record.
- Expert identities, credentials, reviewed content and endorsement authorization.
- Social channel URLs and publication-ready raw-ingredient macro photography.

These unknowns are labelled in the public preview. No certification, medical benefit, clinical review, actual limited edition, product availability or source location is inferred from the concept deck.
