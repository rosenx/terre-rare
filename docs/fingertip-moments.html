Batch No. 01 — from the ingredient’s origin to the bottle.

The human touch / A glimpse of the Terre Rare campaign.

TERRE RARE opens with one story: the journey of Batch No. 01’s rare ingredient, from its origin to the bottle.

Each ingredient comes from a specific place, harvested in its season, in limited quantity. The campaign follows that journey, connecting the material, the bottle and the moment it becomes part of daily care.

A number with meaning

Batch No. 01 — 0032/500 illustrates the campaign’s numbering idea: one unit within a limited run. The number is intended to connect a bottle to its ingredient’s origin and harvest date.

The same story carries across the website, social imagery and posters. Live batch records are not yet published; 0032/500 is a campaign example.
