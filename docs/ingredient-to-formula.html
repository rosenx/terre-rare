From ingredient
to formula.

A botanical name is a beginning. The details give it meaning.

Our brand concept begins with nature, then asks what careful formulation can bring to it. A complete ingredient story should connect the raw material with a clear role in the finished product.

First, identity

A specific ingredient profile begins with a name that can be checked against the finished ingredient list. For a botanical, the plant identity and the part used help make the description precise. Mood photography and natural materials in a campaign are not an ingredient declaration.

Then, origin

Origin gives context to a raw material. The information we intend to share includes a confirmed sourcing region and relevant supplier documentation. Those details need to be available before we describe a source as established.

Finally, purpose

The next question is what the ingredient contributes to a particular formula. Our journal will connect ingredient profiles to product information and any published evidence, with a clear distinction between brand intention and confirmed product facts.

The first profile

The founding ingredient has not yet been identified in the published collection information. Its profile will be added here once its identity, origin and role have been confirmed.

This article explains the brand’s editorial approach. It is not a final ingredient list, efficacy claim or declaration of ingredient origin.
