From ingredient
to formula.

Three botanical ingredients for nourishment, softness and balance.

Moisture Lotion brings together sea buckthorn fruit extract, rose hip seed oil and raspberry seed oil. Each ingredient contributes a distinct role to the care of the skin.

Sea buckthorn fruit extract

Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, sea buckthorn fruit extract helps protect and nourish the skin.

Rose hip seed oil

Rose hip seed oil helps restore skin elasticity and supports a healthy skin barrier.

Raspberry seed oil

Rich in essential fatty acids, raspberry seed oil helps maintain skin softness and a youthful appearance.
